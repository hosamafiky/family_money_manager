Bundled font assets — licensing
================================

Both families below are used under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1.
They are bundled as local assets deliberately: this is an offline-first
application, so typography must not depend on network availability. Do not
replace these with `google_fonts` runtime fetching.

Archivo
  Copyright (c) Omnibus-Type (https://github.com/Omnibus-Type/Archivo)
  License: SIL Open Font License 1.1
  Upstream: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Archivo
  Bundled weights: 400 (Regular), 600 (SemiBold), 800 (ExtraBold)
  Role: Latin script.

IBM Plex Sans Arabic
  Copyright (c) IBM Corp. (https://github.com/IBM/plex)
  License: SIL Open Font License 1.1
  Upstream: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/IBM+Plex+Sans+Arabic
  Bundled weights: 400 (Regular), 600 (SemiBold), 700 (Bold)
  Role: Arabic script.

Full licence text: https://scripts.sil.org/OFL

Why these two, per the design handoff (D1 §05): IBM Plex Sans Arabic is a
naskh-grotesque with genuine lining tabular numerals in both Western and
Arabic-Indic sets, and it shares an x-height with Archivo — so a mixed
Arabic-with-Latin-numerals run, this application's most common string, does
not visibly change font mid-line.
