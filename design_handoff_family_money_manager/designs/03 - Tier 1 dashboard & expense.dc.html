A

Dashboard / Home

What it solves. Audit problems 1, 2 and 5. One dominant region answers "how much can I spend right now", per currency, with no combined total. Held money sits in its own recessed region with a per-reason breakdown, so the 61,500 EGP that today has no dashboard figure finally has one — named, subtotalled, and explicitly outside the hero. The app bar carries two actions instead of five.

Priority order, top to bottom: available to spend → what needs attention → this period's flow → held money → spouse wallet → recent activity → net worth. Quick actions are not in the scroll at all; they live in the pinned bottom bar, which makes them reachable from every scroll position rather than only from the top.

Assumptions in these ten boards
  1. Resolved: the quiet strip is now labelled "إجمالي الأصول" (total assets) with "بدون خصم ديون" beneath it, not "net worth". Net worth is assets − liabilities and liabilities don't exist yet, so calling this net worth would have been the exact kind of number the ledger can't back. Renaming it costs nothing and makes it true today.
  2. Repeat tiles are derived from recent transactions, not a new saved-template entity.
  3. Withdrawn. The "note required when the spender isn't the account owner" rule was mine, not the product's. It is now a non-blocking hint — see the amended B2 note.
  4. Budget consumption is previewed on the review screen. That requires a budget lookup during entry — a provider read, not a write.
  5. The gold row shows quantity only on the dashboard; its EGP estimate lives on the account detail, so no market price appears next to ledger figures.
