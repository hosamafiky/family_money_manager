READ FIRST

The one screen where a user will expect to edit a balance

THE PRESSURE

Every other reconciliation app answers this by letting you overwrite the number.

A user opens their banking app, sees 24,255.00, opens this one and sees 24,300.00. The instinct — trained by every spreadsheet and most finance apps — is to correct the app to match the bank.

This product cannot allow that. Balances are derived from entries (§8.1); there is no balance field to write. So the flow has to convert an instinct to edit into an instinct to record, in the same number of taps, without ever showing a writable balance.

The reframe is one sentence: the difference is not an error in your ledger, it is a transaction you have not recorded yet. Usually a fee.

WHAT IS ACTUALLY NEEDED

One table, one existing operation type, and no schema change to operations.

A statement-balance record is a household's observation of what the bank said on a date. It is evidence, not money: nothing in the ledger derives from it, and deleting every one of them would change no balance.

When it disagrees with the derived balance, the fix is an ordinary adjustment operation with a reason — a normal entry, appended, reversible, and visible in the transaction list like everything else. The reconciliation screen is a route to that entry, not a special write.

So this "special moment" needs no guarded sheet. No third party, no history rewritten, no irreversible step — just an entry the user must not mistake for an edit.

The difference is stated as a missing transaction, in the first sentence, before any number is offered. «مش يعني رصيدنا غلط» is doing the whole job of this screen: it converts "the app is wrong" into "I have something to record", which is the only framing compatible with a derived-balance ledger. The four causes are taps-to-fill, not a category picker — the same pattern as the reversal reasons, for the same reason: a required free-text field produces «فرق» on every entry. «سيبها من غير تسوية» must exist. A user who does not know the cause should not be pushed into inventing one; the observation is kept, the difference stays visible, and the entry waits.

Reconciliation is a habit, so the record of it is the feature. A single check answers "does it match today"; a list answers "when did this account last agree, and what has been unexplained since" — which is the question that matters when something is actually wrong. «فرق مفتوح» is a first-class state. AI2 lets the user walk away without inventing a cause; this screen is where that decision persists instead of evaporating, and the amber note makes clear it is a note, not a debt. The closing line restates the object model — deleting every observation changes no balance — because the history looks enough like a ledger that someone will eventually ask.

Record adjustment ◂ Label flush to the leading edge — left here, right in RTL. Cause chips stay legible: «Bank fees» is a reason, not a figure.

One masking decision this board had to make: does the difference mask? It is the figure a user most wants at a glance and the one most likely to be small and harmless — but it is arithmetic on two masked numbers, so leaving it visible hands back the gap between them, and with either statement in hand the whole balance. So it masks, and what stays is the verdict: matched or not matched, in words. That is the same rule 13 · X3 arrived at from the other side — shares stay because a ratio discloses nothing; a difference goes because a difference is an amount. The cause chips also stay legible: «Bank fees» describes why money moved, not how much.
