H

Accounts — list and creation

The type-selection problem, solved by deleting two options

FinancialAccountType has 12 values. Two of them must not appear in this picker. certificate accounts are created by CreateCertificateUseCase and locked by no_modify_certificate_protected; goalReserve accounts are created by the goal-funding flow and locked by the reserve immutability triggers. A user who hand-made either would get an account the domain can't use — no certificate row behind it, no goal behind it.

So the picker offers ten, and the two absences are explained rather than hidden — because a user who has a certificate and can't find "شهادة" in the list will assume the app is broken. Same principle as the transfer destination exclusion in E1: never let someone reach a validation error the design could have prevented.

First run teaches the three laws, not the features. Every other onboarding in this category lists what the app can do; this one states what it won't do — no balance editing, no deleting, no mixing held with available — because those three surprises are what make users distrust a ledger later. Three steps, a real progress rule, and the currency note pre-empts the question the dashboard will otherwise raise.

Corrected — and the source is better than my design

I drew this as an ordinary income entry and flagged "the category will pollute income reports" as the cost. It doesn't have to. OperationType.openingBalance already exists — «رصيد افتتاحي» — as one of 21 operation types in enum_label_helpers.dart. So an opening balance is its own kind of entry, stays out of income reporting, and needs no new concept. The open question I raised in the footer is answered by the code.

Findings — and the biggest one is good news
The ledger already speaks Tier 3

OperationType has 21 values, and they include liabilityCreation, liabilityRepayment, zakat, sadaqah, goldPurchase, goldSale, assetPurchase, assetSale, interestIncome, certificateFunding, certificateMaturity, goalFunding, goalWithdrawal, childFundDeposit, childFundWithdrawal, openingBalance and adjustment — all with Arabic labels already in the ARB.

Debts, zakat, gold and the reconciliation adjustment are not greenfield: the ledger vocabulary and its localisation exist. That materially reduces the Tier 3 estimate, and it means the adjustment entry in D6's reconciliation row is OperationType.adjustment («تسوية») rather than something new — only the statement-balance record is genuinely new data.

  1. Labels corrected against the ARB. Five of the ten type labels I first drew were wrong: «نقد الأسرة»→«نقدية المنزل», «مدخرات المنزل النقدية»→«مدخرات المنزل», «صندوق طفل محمي»→«صندوق حماية الطفل», «ذهب»→«حيازة الذهب», «استثمار»→«حساب استثمار». All boards now use the source strings.
  2. One label I'd propose changing: accountTypeSpouseCash is «محفظة الزوج/ة». The slash form is correct for a gender-neutral key but reads awkwardly in a list. Since the household knows its own members, the tile should show the account's own name («محفظة هناء») with the type as meta — which is what H1 now does.
  3. Finding: the type picker offers ten of twelve. certificate and goalReserve accounts are created by their own use cases and locked by triggers.
  4. Finding: FundPurpose has 11 values, not the brief's 10 — custom is the eleventh. Three are effectively reserved, so a manual account's purpose picker shows eight.
  5. The spouse wallet is now in H1's spendable region with a «مستبعد من المتاح» chip and is not in the subtotal — it was missing, which contradicted the product's own "one location per amount" thesis.
  6. Finding — two enums have no localization at all. enum_label_helpers.dart defines label functions for nine enums; there is no fundPurposeLabel and no accountOwnerTypeLabel, and a search across lib/core/localization for fundPurpose / ownerType returns zero matches. So «متاح للصرف», «الأسرة» and «مشترك» in H3 are my strings, marked ◂ in the board. This is real localization work — 11 + 5 new ARB keys and two new helper functions — and it is now added to D6 §05.
  7. Report titles corrected to source: «الدخل والمصروف»→«الدخل والمصروفات», «أين يذهب المصروف»→«نسب الإنفاق», and J1's spouse row now uses reportSpouseWalletTitle «محفظة الزوج/ة». settingsTitle «الإعدادات» and reportProtectedFundsTitle «الأموال المحمية» already matched.
  8. Still unread and therefore proposed: the default income category set (TransactionCategory.code) behind I1's chips.
