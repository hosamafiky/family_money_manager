READ FIRST

A column that cannot close, and a note that explains the wrong thing

DEFECT 1 · home savings · arithmetic

The card prints two subsets of transfers and omits the transfers themselves.

The model is explicit: spouseWalletFunding is "Subset of transfersOut to spouse wallets" and spouseWalletReturn is "Subset of transfersIn from spouse wallet returns". HomeSavingsFlowSummary also carries transfersIn and transfersOut in full — and the card renders neither.

So the visible column is opening, income, expense, the two spouse subsets, adjustments, reversal, closing — and it is off by transfers in minus transfers out whenever either exists. There is no lucky case: even a month where the wallet is the only counterparty is wrong, because the money that arrived has no row either.

In our July fixture the gap is 6,000.00 — the transfer in from the salary account, which the card cannot show. AA2 stacks source's rendering against the redesign's on one month.

DEFECT 2 · spouse wallet · the wrong explanation, at the worst moment

When the two totals differ, the card explains it as reversals.

The wallet card ends with if (periodClosingBalance != currentBalance) ReportInfoNote(text: l10n.reportReversalNote) — «آثار العكس معروضة بشكل منفصل.»

But the two figures differ because one is bounded by the period and the other is live. Pick last month and they differ by every transaction since, with no reversal anywhere. The condition fires exactly when the user is most confused and then hands them an unrelated sentence.

The fix is one new key that says what the difference is. The same two totals appear on the home-savings and protected-funds cards with no note at all — so one key serves three reports.

TWO NOUNS GLUED IN CODE

Home savings builds its subset labels as '${reportSpouseWalletTitle} (${reportFunded})' — two translated ARB values joined with a parenthesis at runtime, producing «محفظة الزوج/ة (موّل)».

Worse than 14's (+): that glued a symbol, this glues two nouns and assumes Arabic takes a parenthetical the way English does. Two proposed keys retire it.

FUNDING A WALLET IS NOT INCOME

The wallet card draws reportFunded in Colors.green with a south_west arrow — the same green it uses for income everywhere else. Funding a wallet is a transfer between two household accounts.

And reportReturned is Colors.blue, so on one card the two halves of the same transfer pair get different colours. The redesign makes both achromatic #605D5D.

WHAT SOURCE GETS RIGHT

The spouse-wallet model documents its own identity — opening + funded − spent − returned + reversal = periodClosing — and the card's rows are in exactly that order, so this one can be added up. It is the only report card in the app where that is true.

It also puts reportTransferNote at the top of both screens, which is the right instinct: neither funding nor returning is income or expense, and both screens say so before showing a number.

The two subsets are indented under their parents, and the check says they are not counted twice. Source prints the subsets and drops the parents, which makes the column unaddable; printing both without hierarchy would make it double-count by eye. A 2 px rule and «منها» solve it: the parent carries the arithmetic, the child carries the story. Zeros stay drawn, as in 14 · Y1 — «دخل مباشر 0.00» is a fact about a cash box that received no salary. Every figure here was published in 14's account table; this card is the same account seen through the report that exists to explain the spouse flow, and the blue notice names both counterparties because a transfer with an unnamed other end is half a fact. Source cannot draw this month either: with both parent rows missing it shows 4,000.00 and −2,000.00 against a closing of 8,000.00 — the 6,000.00 that arrived has no row. The board picks «الشهر الماضي» on purpose — it is the only way to see the two totals disagree. On the current month they are equal and the difference is invisible; on any past period they differ by everything since, which is when source fires its reversal note. The replacement sentence names time as the cause and explicitly denies the reversal reading, because a user who has seen «آثار العكس» once will keep looking for a reversal that is not there. Masked bars of different widths are load-bearing: unequal widths tell the reader the account moved, which is exactly as much as privacy mode should give away. The check degrades to a ✓ plus a date, as in 14 · Y3 — the working would hand back the figures.
