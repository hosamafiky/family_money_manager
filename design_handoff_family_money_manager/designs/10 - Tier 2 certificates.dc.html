The escape hatch you
already used up.

Eleven boards — sections P to T. I read certificate.dart, all four presentation screens, account_eligibility.dart and every certificate* value in both ARB files before drawing anything. Six corrections, and one of them reaches back into 05 · Tier 1: the certificate board there offers «إلغاء الشراء», and for this fixture that operation is already impossible. READ FIRST

Six corrections, two of them mine

CORRECTION 1 · reaches back into 05 · F2

«إلغاء الشراء» cannot be offered on this certificate. Profit has already been recorded.

errorCertificateReversalNotAllowedAfterHistory reads, verbatim: «لا يمكن عكس الشراء بعد تسجيل عائد أو استرداد». Our fixture has 2,394.50 of collected profit, so the purchase reversal is closed off — and 05 · F2 draws it as an available third action with an explanation beneath it.

This matters beyond one button. §8.6 of the handoff calls purchase reversal "the escape hatch" for a certificate with no early redemption. The escape hatch expires on the first profit receipt. Between purchase and first profit the certificate can be undone; after it, the money is committed until maturity with no in-app exit at all. That is the honest shape of the feature, and the account board must say so rather than offering the action.

CORRECTION 2 · mine · affects the goals document

I broke the one-fixture rule in the goals boards. Three invented goals where the fixture has one.

07 · L2 establishes it: «مصاريف مدرسة يوسف», reserve 13,600.00 of 13,000.00, 104٪, overfunded by 600.00, target date 2026/07/13 (12 days past), beneficiary يوسف أحمد, four movements, two fundings of 3,400.00 from بنك مصر. Plus «تجديد المطبخ» at 0.00 of 40,000.00 in L3.

My goals document split the same 13,600.00 across two invented goals and added two more. It also asked whether «المستفيد» should be rendered — 07 · L2 already renders it, so that question was answered before I asked it. Both are fixed in the next turn, on these numbers.

The goals findings from source stand unchanged: purpose is localised, creation takes optional initial funding, the orphaned status key, the percent format.

CORRECTION 3 · third occurrence of one bug

Certificate creation and certificate redemption both map AppInsufficientFunds to errorGoalInsufficientReserve — «رصيد احتياطي الهدف غير كافٍ». A goal string, in the certificate flow, naming an account that is not involved.

Goal creation has the same line. Three sites, one cause: there is no per-feature insufficient-funds message. One new key, parameterised by account name, retires all three.

CORRECTION 4 · state values used as field labels

The detail screen labels its lifecycle row with certificateLifecycleActive («نشطة») and its term row with certificateTermActive («ضمن المدة») — the values of those fields, used as their names. And the events list is headed certificatesTitle («الشهادات»).

Three wrong keys on one screen, plus two hardcoded English strings — 'Certificate not found' and 'Error' — that never reach the ARB at all. R1 proposes the four labels it needs.

CORRECTIONS 5 & 6 · deliberate, keep them

certificateRedeemProfitOnly is orphaned. _RedeemMode has only principalOnly and combined; profit-only receipts go to the profit screen, which the redemption screen even links to. Second orphan of the same shape as goalStatusTargetReached — delete both in the same ARB pass.

canRecordProfit is lifecycle != archived — so profit can be recorded against an already-redeemed certificate, and the detail screen offers it. That is correct: a bank can pay a final coupon after the principal has landed. S1 depicts it instead of hiding it.

«مفيش حاجة اتغيرت في السجل» comes first, every time. In a ledger the user's first question on an error is never why — it is did this corrupt anything. A certificate read failure is the sharpest case: the amount at stake is the household's largest single figure, so an unexplained blank where 47,900.00 was is read as loss, not as a network timeout. The shipped screen shows errorGeneric alone; §4's rule — state what happened to the data before offering retry — makes this a new key rather than a rewording. The detail screen is worse: 'Certificate not found' and 'Error' are hardcoded English and never reach the ARB. Every line of commentary lives here, not in the frame: a designer note rendered inside a phone frame is neither source copy nor proposed copy, so it would sit outside the provenance rule.

The privacy contract, proved on the largest figure in the product. Every amount becomes an ink bar per digit group at 0.62 em, keeping its currency code and its layout width; the held region keeps its recessed fill, its 2 px rules, its hatched edge and «PRINCIPAL · NOT SPENDABLE». A masked certificates screen still answers can I spend this — which is the whole test. Digit groups are the point: 47,900.00 is three groups and masks as three bars, so the reader keeps the magnitude the contract deliberately preserves and loses only the digits. One long bar would hide how many digits there are, which is more than the rule asks for. Two masking decisions worth stating: the collected-profit bars stay income-tinted and keep the +, because direction is not a secret and the sign and colour are the accessible half of the row; and the term progress bar is not masked at all, since it encodes elapsed days. The LTR check also catches the term prefixes — «الحالة» / «المدة» become STATUS / TERM in the same small-caps treatment 03 · A6 uses, so the two proposed keys need both locales, not just an Arabic string.

The rate field is the trap. Two figures, then their sum, in that order — and the sum is labelled as a bank deposit, not as a total. The domain comment is unambiguous, and this is the one screen where the mistake is easy: 48,698.50 lands in one account on one day, and a reasonable person would file it as income. Printing it third, after both parts have been classified, is what stops that. Redemption is irreversible — errorCertificateRedemptionReversalNotSupported — which makes review mandatory rather than a courtesy, and makes «راجع الأرقام قبل ما تكمّل» the only place in this document where the copy asks the user to slow down. It is also why this flow gets a review screen but not a GuardedConfirmSheet: there is no third party, no reason to justify, and nothing to acknowledge — only arithmetic to check.
