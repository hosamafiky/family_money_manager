Family Money Manager · D3 · Tier 2 · turn 4 — goals, rebuilt

One goal. I drew three.

Twelve boards, sections M to O — the two screens owed since turn two, plus the guarded flow. These letters sit before 10's P–T because these boards were drawn first; the numbers on them were not. This version is rebuilt on 07 · L2's fixture and on the goals source read in turn three: goal_creation_screen.dart, goals_list_screen.dart, complete_goal_params.dart, account_eligibility.dart, every goal* / purpose* value in both ARB files, and — for the correction panel below — goal_detail_screen.dart and release_goal_screen.dart. goal_use_cases.dart has not been opened; its creation parameters were read through the screen's call site.

READ FIRST

What this version fixes, and what it keeps

THE ERROR · mine

I invented three goals and split the established reserve across them.

07 · L2 fixes it precisely: «مصاريف مدرسة يوسف», reserve 13,600.00 of 13,000.00, 104٪, overfunded by 600.00, target date 2026/07/13 — thirteen days past as of 2026/07/26, where 07 printed twelve on the 25th — beneficiary يوسف أحمد, four movements, fundings of 3,400.00 from بنك مصر. Four of those at 3,400.00 is exactly 13,600.00, so N2 and N3 draw the first one and the arithmetic closes without a new figure.

The second goal is «تجديد المطبخ» at 0.00 of 40,000.00, from 07 · L3's funding sheet. Two goals, one holding money — which is why the held region and the goals total are the same number.

I also asked whether «المستفيد» should be rendered or deleted. 07 · L2 already renders it. The question was answered before I asked it; N1 renders the field.

WHAT STANDS · read from source, unchanged

Four findings survive the rebuild. Two of them contradict the handoff.

GoalPurpose is fully localised — seven purpose* keys in both locales. Handoff §9 says it has "no localization at all", which drops the new-key count from 23 to 16.

Creation takes optional initial funding — initialFundingSourceAccountId and initialFundingMinorUnits, source filtered by AccountEligibility.isGoalFundingSource. My first version claimed creation never moves money. It can, and N2 draws it.

goalPercent is «{percent}٪ ممول» — Arabic percent sign, an int parameter, and percentageFunded is nullable. 07 already used 104٪; every board here matches.

goalStatusTargetReached is orphaned and value-identical to goalProgressTargetReached («تم بلوغ الهدف»). Delete it with certificateRedeemProfitOnly in one ARB pass.

THE GUARD, CORRECTED BY THE FIXTURE

My first version put early completion on a goal with money in it. On this fixture that is impossible: «مصاريف مدرسة يوسف» is over its target, so completing it is the normal path — errorGoalNormalCompletionRequiresTarget is already satisfied and no guard applies. The only goal that can complete early is «تجديد المطبخ», which holds 0.00.

That turns out to be the more useful pair. O1 guards a goal with nothing to release — so the arithmetic block disappears and the consequence is the whole shortfall — and O2 completes a goal normally, with no reason and no acknowledgement. Side by side they show what the guard is for, which one board could not.

STILL OWED BY L10N · three sites, one cause

Goal creation maps AppInsufficientFunds to errorGoalInsufficientReserve — «رصيد احتياطي الهدف غير كافٍ» — although at creation the reserve is empty by definition and the shortfall is in the source account. Certificate creation and redemption do the same. One parameterised message retires all three.

And the submit button reuses goalNew — «هدف جديد», the screen's own title — so the primary action is a noun. Same defect as the certificate's «شهادة جديدة». Both need a verb key.

ADDED AFTER READING THE GOAL DETAIL AND RELEASE SCREENS

The guarded flow is bypassed in source — and its reason ships in English

THE FINDING · it validates section O rather than changing it

«تعيين كمكتمل» on the detail screen writes an early completion with no sheet, no reason and no acknowledgement from the user.

The button's handler calls the use case directly with earlyCompletion: true, earlyCompletionConfirmed: true and earlyCompletionReason: 'Completed from goal detail screen' — a hardcoded English string that lands in an Arabic-first ledger as the household's stated reason for closing a goal short.

The two fields CompleteGoalParams documents as user obligations — "required and non-empty", "enforces explicit acknowledgment" — are satisfied by the caller on the user's behalf. The domain's guard is intact; the UI walks around it.

O1–O3 need no revision. They are what this button must become, and this is the evidence that the sheet is a fix rather than a flourish.

FOUR MORE, ONE OF WHICH CONFIRMS O2

1 · Every completion is recorded as early. The flag is true unconditionally, so a goal that has reached its target — «مصاريف مدرسة يوسف» — closes as an early completion with that English reason attached. O2's unguarded normal path does not exist in source at all.

2 · A completed goal can still release. canRelease = status != archived, and archive requires reserveBalance == 0. O2's amber notice was right — completion does not free the money, and source agrees.

3 · Goals have revisions. progress.revisions carries name, target and a revisionReason per change — an append-only history of the goal's definition that no board in this set draws. 07 · L2 shows movements only.

4 · Two more wrong keys. The movements section is headed goalMovementFunding («تمويل»), and the percentage row uses goalPercent as a label with the progress state as its value — «104٪ ممول : ممول بزيادة». The error state renders e.toString() straight to the user.

5 · release_goal_screen.dart, read too — three English validators. The destination and amount fields validate with the literal strings 'Required', 'Required' and 'Must be > 0', rendered under the field to an Arabic-first user. Third site of the hardcoded-English class, after this screen's completion reason and the certificate detail's 'Certificate not found'. The release reason is properly validated against errorGoalReleaseReasonEmpty, and the destination list correctly excludes the goal's own reserve — and this is the one screen where errorGoalInsufficientReserve is the right key, which is what makes its three uses elsewhere misuses rather than a naming quibble.

The shipped list is a Card per goal with a LinearProgressIndicator and three unlabelled figures in a spaceBetween row — reserve, percent, target. The arithmetic makes the fixture close on itself. O

Completion — three boards, and why only one is guarded

CompleteGoalParams is explicit: earlyCompletionConfirmed "must be true when earlyCompletion is true", earlyCompletionReason is "required and non-empty", and idempotencyKey defaults to the goal id — so a double tap is absorbed while a second attempt with a different reason returns AppDuplicateConflict. Normal completion carries none of that. On this fixture the overfunded goal completes normally and the empty one can only complete early, which puts the two paths side by side.

A guarded sheet with no arithmetic is the interesting case. Everything the protected-withdrawal pattern relies on — before, amount, after — would print as zeros here, and a column of 0.00 reads as a broken screen rather than as "no money moves". So the arithmetic block is replaced by one labelled statement, and the largest figure on the sheet becomes the shortfall: 40,000.00, the whole target, because that is the entire consequence. The guard still applies — reason and acknowledgement are domain requirements, not proportional to the amount. What it deliberately does not have: the tinted header, the coloured frame, biometric re-auth and the heavy haptic, all of which stay exclusive to the protected withdrawal and the reversal. Nothing else in the app has a coloured frame, and if a second flow borrows it the frame stops meaning "someone else's money".
