Family Money Manager · D6 · Implementation handoff and migration

Every token, every phase,
and what actually breaks.

Written against app_theme.dart and app_theme_extensions.dart read in full — not the brief's summary of them. Three facts in those files change the plan materially, and they're the first thing below. A prep phase plus nine, each shippable. Every existing role name preserved.

01

Three facts the brief didn't mention, and they matter

The brief listed the token role names and their current values. It did not say how those values are produced, and that turns out to be the whole shape of the migration.

FACT 1

Eight of the sixteen colour roles are derived, not literal.

The brief quoted seven hex values and implied the rest were the same kind of thing. They aren't. primaryAction, neutralInfo, mainSurface, secondarySurface, divider, primaryText, secondaryText and disabled all read off ColorScheme.fromSeed(0xFF1A6B3C) — scheme.primary, scheme.surface, scheme.outlineVariant and so on. disabled isn't even a colour, it's onSurface.withValues(alpha: 0.38).

Consequence: you cannot drop the new palette in as values. Either the seed changes (and every derived tone moves with it, including the eight roles above and every stock M3 widget), or those eight roles stop deriving and take literals. I recommend literals: the Modernist ground is a warm near-grey that M3's tonal algorithm will not produce from any seed, and leaving surfaces derived means the design is at the mercy of Material's palette generator. Phase 1 makes all sixteen literal; the ColorScheme stays for stock widgets and gets a neutral seed.

FACT 2

The type scale is nothing like what the redesign needs, and two roles move violently.

Current sectionTitle is 18 px / w600. The redesign's is 13 px / w800 / uppercase — because Modernist's section heading is a small tracked label above a 2 px rule, not a heading. That's a 28% shrink.

In the other direction, reportValue is currently 15 px / w500 and becomes 20 px / w800 — a 33% growth, because it carries the secondary-currency rows in the balance hero.

Also: AppTextRoles.fromScheme sets no font family at all. Adding Archivo and IBM Plex Sans Arabic isn't a value change, it's a signature change — the factory has to know the locale. See §04.

FACT 3

There is no "ground" role — the scaffold uses mainSurface.

scaffoldBackgroundColor: financial.mainSurface. So today the page background and a card are the same colour, and card separation comes from a 12 px radius plus a 50%-alpha outline. The redesign needs three distinct surfaces — ground, main, recessed — because the held-money region is the recessed one.

Three new fields are unavoidable: ground, recessedSurface and focusRing. Those are the only additions to AppFinancialColors in the whole redesign — all 16 existing names stay, nothing is renamed, and adding is additive so no call site breaks.

Also worth knowing: space2 = 2.0 and space48 = 48.0 exist, marked deprecated. They're off the 4/8/12/16/20/24/32/40 scale. Phase 2 deletes them.

The signature change nobody can avoid

AppTextRoles.fromScheme(ColorScheme) has no locale parameter and sets no fontFamily. Two families with different metrics per role cannot be expressed through it.

factory AppTextRoles.forLocale(
  ColorScheme scheme,
  Locale locale,
)

fromScheme stays as a deprecated delegate that assumes en, so no call site breaks on day one. AppTheme.light() / .dark() gain a locale argument, which means MaterialApp must rebuild its theme on locale change — a real behavioural change worth testing deliberately.

Fonts must be bundled, not fetched. This is an offline-first app; google_fonts at runtime would make typography depend on connectivity. Add both families to pubspec.yaml as assets — Archivo 400/600/800 and IBM Plex Sans Arabic 400/600/700, six files.

04

Migration — phase 0 plus nine, each one shippable

Ordered so that the riskiest thing in each phase is the only risky thing in it. Phase 0 ships byte-identical output; phases 1–3 are token-only; 4 onwards is screens. Typography is deliberately alone in phase 3 — it is the one change that moves layout metrics everywhere, and mixing it with anything else makes a failing test impossible to diagnose.

Two things I'd want confirmed before phase 1
  1. Literals over a new seed. I recommend the eight derived roles take fixed values and ColorScheme keeps a neutral seed purely for stock M3 widgets. The alternative — reseeding and letting Material generate the surfaces — is less code but gives up control of the exact ground the whole design is calibrated against.
  2. Are there golden/screenshot tests? I've assumed yes and that phase 1 regenerates them. If the 1,594 tests are all widget-and-unit with no goldens, phase 1 is nearly free and phase 3 is the only expensive one — which would let 1 and 2 ship together.
