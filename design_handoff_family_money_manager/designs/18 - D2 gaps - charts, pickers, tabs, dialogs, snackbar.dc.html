Family Money Manager · D2 — the six gaps

Six components the
screens kept assuming.

Seven boards, section AJ. Every screen document so far has leaned on one of these and drawn it inline: a share bar, a date field, a chip row, a sheet. This closes them as components. Three are genuinely new (the chart primitives). Three already exist as Material defaults — showDatePicker, AlertDialog, SnackBar — which is worse than missing, because a default is a decision nobody made.

What the source actually uses today

CHARTS · none exist

All seven report screens are read and not one draws a chart. The only graphical element in the feature is LinearProgressIndicator, used in the goals list and in a per-row loading state.

So the three primitives are additive, and they arrive into a product whose rule is already written: every chart is preceded by the table it draws. Nothing has to be un-taught.

DATES · stock Material, three call sites

showDatePicker in goal creation and twice in certificate creation; showDateRangePicker in the report period selector. Every one is the framework's rounded, elevated, centred dialog — radius, elevation and centred text all contradict §2.

Its month and weekday names come from Flutter's own localisation, not from the ARB — so they are outside the string-provenance rule this project has enforced on every other word.

DIALOGS & SNACKBARS · errors that vanish

Household uses three AlertDialogs; the archive confirmation's entire body is the member's name. Goal creation, certificate creation and household all report failures through ScaffoldMessenger.showSnackBar.

A snackbar auto-dismisses. «يوجد زوج/ة مسجل مسبقاً» and «رصيد احتياطي الهدف غير كافٍ» are both delivered by something designed to be missed.

Line charts get points, not curves: a smoothed line asserts values between measurements, and in a ledger every value between two月-end snapshots is a fiction. These three are additive — no report draws a chart today, so nothing has to be replaced.

Three switching controls, distinguished by what happens when they overflow. The bottom bar cannot scroll, so five labelled tabs is a hard ceiling and the sixth is what pushed Reports into «المزيد» (D4). A segmented control cannot scroll either — if the options do not fit, it is the wrong control. Chips scroll, which is exactly why they may filter but never navigate: an off-screen destination is an invisible one. Selection is ink and a 3 px rule, never a tint, so the state survives greyscale and privacy mode. No horizontal TabBar appears in any screen read, and this board proposes that none is introduced.

Three controls, three different mirroring answers, which is why they needed one board. The bottom bar does not mirror: its five destinations are a fixed sequence, and flipping them would move Home under the user's other thumb between locales. Segments do: three options read as one sentence, so «الكل» leads in RTL and "All" leads in LTR. The calendar grid mirrors as a block while weekday initials stay in their own reading order. And one masking rule this document had to settle: dates never mask. Nothing is reconstructable from a date, and a masked calendar is an unusable chooser protecting nothing — the same reasoning that keeps 13 · X3's shares visible and 17 · AI4's difference hidden.

One test decides which: can the answer be yes or no with nothing to read and nothing to fill? If yes, a dialog; otherwise a sheet. That puts discard-draft in a dialog and everything else in the product — reversal, guarded withdrawal, early completion, funding, reconciliation, pickers — in a sheet. Three features report failures through a component designed to be missed. Goal creation, certificate creation and household all call ScaffoldMessenger.showSnackBar for validation and conflict errors; a snackbar auto-dismisses, cannot be re-read, and on a slow save may appear after the user has looked away. So the rule is one line: snackbars confirm successful writes, nothing else. Failures render at their cause — beneath the field, or in the panel that requested the operation — and persist until resolved. The one permitted action is «أضف حركة عكسية», which the handoff fixes as the post-save affordance precisely because it is not an undo: there is no undo, and a snackbar that offered one would be the app's largest lie.
