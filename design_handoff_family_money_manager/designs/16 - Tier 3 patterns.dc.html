Family Money Manager · D3 · Tier 3 — patterns, not every pixel

Six features the ledger
already knows the words for.

Six boards, one per pattern, sections AC to AH. None of these features has a folder in lib/features/ — I enumerated all seventeen: accounts, balance, budgets, certificates, dashboard, foundation_detail, goals, household, ledger, more, onboarding, planning, reports, settings, shell, smoke_screen, transactions. No gold, no liabilities, no zakat, no lock. But OperationType ships nine labels for them — «شراء ذهب» «بيع ذهب» «إنشاء التزام» «سداد التزام» «زكاة» «صدقة» «شراء أصل» «بيع أصل» «دخل فائدة» — all localised in both files. The vocabulary exists; the screens do not.

AC · AD

Gold, and debts

Two features that break a rule the product has held since 01: gold is a quantity that is not money, and a liability is a negative that the app has never had to draw.

Three numbers that must never be one number. The quantity (50.000 g) is what you own; the ledger cost (196,000.00) is what you paid, and it is in the ledger as two goldPurchase transfers; the estimate (242,500.00) is quantity × a price you typed, and it is in nothing. The entry date is the honesty. «من 6 أيام» is printed beside the estimate because a stale price is the only way this figure lies, and the client's decision was manual pricing precisely so the app never implies a live market. Gold is a quantity row in the currency ledger (handoff §3): it sits below the currency rows with its unit, and no total spans it. What is not presentation work: a gold-holdings table with a gram price and its entry date. The two operation types are already localised.

The tab that cost Reports its place (D4): debts have due dates, reports do not, and six labelled tabs truncate at 200% scale. The three-way split is the whole feature. liabilityRepayment is one operation and three different kinds of money — principal is a transfer that reduces what you owe, interest and fees are ordinary expenses. Collapsing them is the single most common error in household budgeting apps, and it inflates the expense total every month by the principal. The remaining balance is derived, like every balance in this product: «محسوب من قيود السداد، مش رقم مخزّن». And this is the precondition for «صافي الثروة» — until liabilities exist the dashboard strip stays «إجمالي الأصول», which is the rename this project made in turn one.

The feature is a refusal with a table attached. Zakat depends on the nisab, the lunar year, what counts as zakatable and how debt is treated — all of which are matters of scholarly judgement that differ between people. An app that prints «زكاتك 2,525.00» has quietly issued a fatwa. So the screen shows the figures a person or their sheikh would ask for — assets, what is held, what is protected, what is owed, and the gold estimate flagged as an estimate — and stops. The two operation types already exist and are already localised, which means zakat and sadaqah are filterable and reportable from day one without a category. The protected line is there for a reason: a child's money is the classic case where the answer differs by school, and the app must surface it rather than silently include or exclude it.

One rule holds the whole pattern: the microphone fills a form, it does not write a row. The proposal lands in the same review screen the numpad produces, subject to the same validation, the same budget preview and the same «مش بيتحذف» consequence line — so there is exactly one path into the ledger and voice is a faster way to reach it, not a second one. Uncertainty is shown as uncertainty. A field the model guessed at low confidence is amber and asks; it is never silently filled with the most likely value, because a wrong spender on a household expense is invisible until the attribution report is wrong. Egyptian numerals spoken aloud — «مية وتسعين» — resolve to 190.00 in Western digits, matching the numeral decision in §2. Not presentation work: speech-to-text, and a proposal model that returns per-field confidence rather than a single sentence.

Two mechanisms, one settings screen, and a sentence separating them. They get conflated because both are "hiding", but they answer different threats: the lock is about a phone in someone else's hands, privacy mode is about a phone in yours with someone reading over your shoulder. Different triggers, different durations, different affordances — and the guarded withdrawal's re-auth is a third thing again, which the board says so nobody merges them later. The masked specimen is the privacy contract, drawn once. Digits become an ink bar per group; region, rule, hatch, lock and label survive. Every masked board in this project — 10 · P5, 11 · M6, 12 · U6, 13 · X3, 14 · Y3, 15 · AB2, and later 17 · AI4, 18 · AJ4b and 19 · AK4b — is an instance of this one specimen. Privacy mode does not auto-arm on resume, because a mode that turns itself on trains the user to turn it off, and a habitually-dismissed mask protects nobody.

Tier 3 · six patterns · 68 boards in the set

Six boards: AC1, AD1, AE1, AF1, AG1, AH1. One per pattern, as the brief asked — enough to fix the rules, not enough to build from. Each names what is not presentation work.

Schema and query work these six imply: a gold-holdings table with quantity, karat, a manual gram price and its entry date; a liabilities feature with six debt types and a principal / interest / fee split per repayment; a month-end asset and liability snapshot for the net-worth series; a speech-to-text pipeline returning per-field confidence; and lock settings plus a privacy flag. No new operation types — all nine needed already exist and are localised in both files.

What is left: bank reconciliation · the D2 gaps (chart primitives, date and category pickers, tabs, dialog and sheet anatomy, snackbar) · D5 adaptive last. Plus the two confirming reads: the three goal screens against 07 · L2–L3, and categoryLabelFromCode against 13 · X1.

That is all. Every screen in this document is greenfield, so bucket 1 is nine words long and bucket 2 is nearly everything. Mine · marked ◂The mark is exhaustive. Every screen title, every field label, every explanatory sentence, every state name, and the two settings screens in full. If a framed string has no ◂ and is not one of the nine operation labels, that is a defect in this document. Not copy, so unmarked: fixture data — account, member and product names, amounts, dates, quantities, and text the household typed or spoke, including AG1's transcript «صرفت مية وتسعين جنيه بقالة من محفظة هناء النهاردة» — and the English annotation outside the frames.
