A count I got wrong, and a filter nobody can reach

CORRECTION 1 · mine · reaches into 08 · D4 and the handoff

There are seven report screens, not nine.

reports_landing_screen.dart builds exactly seven entries: الدخل والمصروفات · نسب الإنفاق · الفئات · تدفقات الحسابات · مدخرات المنزل · محفظة الزوج/ة · الأموال المحمية. There are seven matching use cases, seven screens, and an eighth screen that is not a report — report_transaction_list_screen.dart, the shared drill-down.

"The nine reports" appears in the handoff §11, in the migration plan's phase 9, and in every status line I have written since. It was never counted from source. Seven reports + one drill-down = eight screens, which is also what D4's route table should say.

Consequence for D6: phase 9 is smaller than costed, and it is the phase that also carries the nav change.

DEFECT · the whole filter is unreachable

ReportFilter has eight dimensions. No screen offers a single control for any of them.

Accounts, owner types, spenders, beneficiaries, scopes, categories, operation types, currencies — plus includeReversed and protectedOnly. The queries honour all of it. ReportPeriodSelector is the only control that exists, and it sets the period, never the filter.

So every report answers one question — "this month, everything" — and the by-spender, by-category and by-scope sections are the only way to slice anything. includeReversed defaults to true and cannot be turned off, which is why gross figures are the ones on screen.

The redesign does not add eight pickers. Each breakdown row becomes the filter — tap a category, get that category's transactions — because the filter the domain already supports is exactly the drill-down the UI is missing.

§4's BIDI BUG, WITH AN ADDRESS

ReportAmountText.formatMinorUnits returns '$currencyCode $sign$major.$minor' — the currency code leads, the sign is an ASCII hyphen, and there are no tabular figures and no isolation. In an RTL paragraph that renders as EGP 382.50-.

Its Semantics label is '$text $currencyCode', so a screen reader hears the currency twice. Every report amount in the app goes through this one method, which makes it the highest-leverage fix in phase 4.

HARDCODED COLOUR, ON THE WRONG AXIS

Colors.green, Colors.red, Colors.orange and Colors.grey appear literally in report_widgets.dart and in all three screens read here. AppFinancialColors is never consulted.

Green-versus-red is precisely the axis §2 moved away from, and here it is the only thing separating income from expense: both are positive numbers, and the category rows carry no sign at all.

DELETING report_widgets.dart — CONFIRMED, WITH ONE CARVE-OUT

Six of its seven widgets are re-implementations of things that must be shared: amount text, labelled amount row, section header, loading, error, empty. The handoff's instruction holds.

The seventh is not report-specific either. ReportPeriodSelector drives DashboardPeriod and duplicates the dashboard's own period chips. It moves to a shared component rather than being deleted — otherwise the dashboard and the reports drift apart on the one control they must agree on.

Seven categories sum to 10,959.25, the gross expense from W1 — and the amber row is where the two reports diverge, labelled «فيها معكوسة» with its count reading «كلها معكوسة». Naming the discrepancy costs two proposed strings and removes the only way a careful user could conclude the app cannot add. Every percentage in this document truncates, like percentageUsed and goalPercent and like 10 · P1's 36٪ — so the seven category shares read 27 · 21 · 13 · 11 · 10 · 8 · 5 and sum to 95, not 100. A share set that adds to exactly 100 is a rounded set, and rounding here would put 11٪ on «مدرسة» in one report and 10٪ on «طفل» in the next for the same 1,200.00. The bars stay proportional to the untruncated share; only the printed figure truncates. A per-row share bar in ink, not a pie: shares of one total on a common baseline are comparable, wedges are not, and ink means the bar carries no judgement about the category. Every row is a filter.
